# Physical Extremes community

Physical Extremes exists to turn ambitious engineering questions into inspectable reasoning, reproducible work, and better questions.

## How to participate

- Use **Report Q&A** to interrogate a report's equations, assumptions, or system boundary.
- Use **Technical Critique** when evidence may require a correction or revision.
- Use **Investigation Proposals** to nominate a bounded question for the Open Problems Registry.
- Use **Build Logs** to share simulations, prototypes, experiments, and useful failures.

Confirmed corrections should become issues or pull requests. Open-ended inquiry should remain in Discussions until it produces a tractable change.

## Engineering standard

Separate measurement, modeling, inference, and speculation. Cite primary evidence when it exists. State operating envelopes and omitted effects. Make uncertainty visible. Critique claims rather than people, and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, personal attacks, fabricated evidence, spam, and unsafe instructions presented without appropriate context may be removed. Repeated or severe violations may lead to restricted participation.

## Proposing a report

A strong proposal contains a precise question, governing constraint, current evidence, capability unlocked, and one realistic next step. The goal is not to sound futuristic; the goal is to make the future testable.
