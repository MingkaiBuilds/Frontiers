# Contributing to Physical Extremes

Physical Extremes™ is an owner-published engineering archive founded and created by Mingkai. Public participation is welcomed through technical questions, critiques, investigation proposals, and build logs. The report studio and publication authority remain private to the owner.

## Where to contribute

- Use GitHub Discussions for questions, evidence, proposed investigations, and build logs.
- Use issues for confirmed defects in the website or clearly scoped corrections.
- Do not submit an unsolicited report or editorial article as a pull request. A named collaboration should be agreed with Mingkai before report authorship begins.

You retain ownership of copyrightable material that you submit.

By submitting material to this repository, including through Discussions, issues, or pull requests, you grant Mingkai a nonexclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to host, reproduce, quote, summarize, edit, adapt, publish, and display that contribution as part of Physical Extremes™ and its associated archives. You also permit Mingkai to identify you by the name or account attached to the submission.

This permission allows useful community work to become part of a report revision or public research record. It is not a transfer of your ownership. When a contribution is materially incorporated, Physical Extremes will provide reasonable attribution unless you request anonymity.

By submitting, you represent that you created the material or have the right to provide it under these terms. Do not submit confidential information, trade secrets, export-controlled material, third-party content without permission, or enabling details for dangerous activity without appropriate safeguards and context.

## Editorial control

Mingkai retains final editorial and publication control. A submission may be declined, edited, summarized, or removed for technical quality, safety, relevance, rights, or community-conduct reasons. Participation does not create a partnership, employment relationship, joint authorship, or right to use the Physical Extremes™ identity.
